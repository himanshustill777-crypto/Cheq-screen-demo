Important Terms

Is 80% bill coverage guaranteed?

No. Funding requires eligibility checks and approval from an authorized lending partner.

Does a ₹25,000 FD guarantee credit?

No. FD creation, lien, credit eligibility, interest and withdrawal rules depend on the actual partner bank.

Can CheQ remove bank penalties?

CheQ may assist with waiver requests; banks decide whether charges can be reversed.

Can CheQ debit money automatically?

Only with valid explicit customer authorization under applicable mandate rules. Repayment dates and charges must be disclosed in advance.

How do I report card fraud?

Contact your card issuer immediately. In India, report cyber fraud via 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
